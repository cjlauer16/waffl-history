## SEASON STORYLINES

**The deadline arms race.** cjlauer16 added Swift and Lawrence, loganstahl71 added Likely and Evans, and Winnie24 added Kittle. The top four are all-in, and the trade deadline is next week (Week 12). The strangest move came from smurphy2121, who sold three starters while tied for a playoff spot and then won anyway. Either it's a long-term plan or they enjoy chaos. Possibly both.

**cjlauer16 has clinched a playoff spot.** At 20-2 with six results left, only loganstahl71 and stevenbaker58 can even reach 20 wins. cjlauer16 has won seven straight and still has the third-luckiest record in the league: their head-to-head win rate (.909) is 109 points above their all-play rate (.800). Being good *and* lucky is a dangerous combination.

**The 12-10 logjam.** gcmgazelle, Pogogo60, and smurphy2121 are all 12-10, and points for is the only thing deciding who's in: gcmgazelle has 1340.04, Pogogo60 1271.44, and smurphy2121 1263.66. That's 7.78 points between sixth and seventh, over eleven weeks. Every bench decision from here on matters.

**The quarterback carousel.** Four teams went shopping for QBs this week: cjlauer16 (Lawrence), smurphy2121 (Bryce Young), Pogogo60 (Nix and Cam Ward), and jkrempa (Watson, Lock, and Herbert). Meanwhile, ktomaset found stability with Geno Smith, and bmiller99 picked the right one. Week 11 was a strange week.

**The MARC Train has its first winning streak.** Two straight wins after a 0-6 stretch, and the bench went from a season-record 57.1 points to 6.5. They're 6-16 and realistically out of the race, but every team they beat from here on is a team that needed the win.
