**#1 vs. #2 is next week.** cjlauer16 (18-2, six straight wins) meets loganstahl71 (16-4) in Week 11. cjlauer16 has the best all-play record in the league (87-22). loganstahl71 has the luckiest schedule in the league: their head-to-head win rate (.800) beats their all-play rate (.664) by more than any other team's, and they've had the fewest points scored against them. One of these streaks is ending.

**bmiller99's season is now a bench-management case study.** Five losses their own optimal lineup would have won (Weeks 5, 7, 8, 9, and 10), a six-game head-to-head losing streak, and **$88 of unspent FAAB**, the most in the league. Fix those five lineups and bmiller99 would be in the top four instead of three games out.

**The bubble is tied.** gcmgazelle and Pogogo60 are both 10-10, and gcmgazelle holds sixth only on points for (1183.38 to 1151.72). Pogogo60 has made **51 pickups** this season and has **$21 of FAAB** left, the least in the league. gcmgazelle lost the median by 0.27 this week. Four weeks and eight results left, and it may come down to decimals.

**smurphy2121 is the unluckiest team in the league, and is climbing anyway.** Their head-to-head win rate (.400) trails their all-play rate (.582) by the most of anyone, and only three teams have had more points scored against them. They've still won two straight to climb to #5. If the luck evens out, nobody in the bottom half wants to see them.

**Season records keep falling.** CMC's 44.3 is the top started game of the year, Gibbs's 40.7 is third, smurphy2121's 165.78 is the third-best team score, and Pickens's 38.0 is now the best *benched* performance of the season. That last one is a record nobody wants.
